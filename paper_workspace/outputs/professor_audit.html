01 · Executive verdict

The project is technically credible, scientifically bounded.

The repository is an audited independent reconstruction of the available Beltrão-inspired workflow and a controlled HPSS replacement experiment. It is not an exact paper reproduction, and it is not a clinical validation study.

Professor-level conclusion: Both NMF and HPSS genuinely improve over raw NLS (reducing PAPER_18 RMSE from 7.49 bpm to 6.86 and 6.80 bpm, respectively), and HPSS achieves equivalent accuracy across all four cohort variants while running 6.3–6.9× faster. The result remains exploratory because the original cohort is unresolved, the 18-session subset is reference-validity ranked, the NMF comparator often hits its 500-iteration limit, and no untouched external or clinical validation exists.

02 · What the code does

One shared pipeline, two artifact-mitigation branches.

Baseline branch

rbm_nmf.py uses envelope normalization, an 11-component NNDSVD-initialized NMF model, adaptive RBM filtering (α = 3.0, sparsity 0.6), Wiener ratio masking, phase reuse, and overlap-add reconstruction. It records iterations and convergence instead of hiding warnings.

HPSS branch

hpss.py uses envelope normalization, zero-frequency centered (fftshift) time/frequency median filters (l_harm = 7, l_perc = 21), and a soft harmonic mask. It is deterministic under the locked settings and uses the same downstream NLS estimator as NMF.

06 · Critical interpretation

What could still mislead a reader?

Critical threats

  1. Original paper cohort IDs are not verified.
  2. The active 18-session subset is selected by reference validity.
  3. No untouched external dataset has been evaluated.
  4. No clinical utility, safety, or target-hardware endpoint exists.

Important limitations

  1. NMF convergence is low under the active 500-iteration setting.
  2. Several paper details are inferred, including some solver and synchronization choices.
  3. Measurements are clustered within patients.
  4. Zero-valid sessions and missingness may be informative rather than random.

Claims that must not appear in the presentation

Do not say: “We exactly reproduced Beltrão et al.”, “HPSS is clinically superior,” “HPSS is proven better,” or “non-significant differences prove equivalence.”

Say instead: “We produced an audited independent reconstruction; HPSS was faster and showed a promising but not definitive internal accuracy signal.”
