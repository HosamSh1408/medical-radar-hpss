## 2.1 Continuous-Wave Quadrature Doppler Radar Physics (`WP1`, Cluster 2)
- **RF Backscatter & Slow-Time Phase Modulation:** Derive received complex quadrature channels $\iqI, \iqQ$ at $\carrierFreq = 24\,\mathrm{GHz}$ ($\radarLambda = 12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$), resting standoff $\nomRange$, residual phase $\phaseZero$, I/Q gain imbalance $\iqGainImb$, quadrature skew $\iqPhaseSkew$, and DC offsets $\iqDcI, \iqDcQ$ (`droitcour2004range`, `park2007arctangent`, `li2013review`, `hu2014noncontact`).
- **Jacobi–Anger Bessel Harmonic Expansion:** For sinusoidal chest displacement $\chestDisp = A_r \sin(2\pi f_b t)$ with modulation index $\modIndex = 4\pi A_r / \radarLambda$, expand baseband phase modulation into Bessel series $\besselJ{n}{\modIndex}$ (`morgan2009novel`, `wu2013phase`, `li2008random`), proving analytically why harmonic/intermodulation products ($2f_b, 3f_b, f_h \pm f_b$) arise inherently at $24\,\mathrm{GHz}$ even before motion artifacts (`tu2013respiration`, `naishadham2016estimation`).
- **Complex Signal Demodulation (`CSD`) vs. Unwrapped Arctangent (`AD`) vs. `DACM`:** Prove why per-window mean centering $\csdSignal = (b_I - \bar{b}_I) + j(b_Q - \bar{b}_Q)$ (`beltrao2022contactless`, `li2008random`) eliminates $\phaseZero$ null points while preserving exact $SO(2)\times\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ amplitude/rotation equivariance (critical for downstream `filter` invariance), whereas naive arctangent (`demod.ad`, audit `A10`) and extended DACM (`wang2014noncontact`) distort without accurate ellipse center fitting (`park2007arctangent`, `fitzgibbon1999direct`, `zakrzewski2014quadrature`, `singh2013data`).
