## 1.1 Clinical Motivation & Contactless NICU Respiratory Monitoring
- **Preterm Neonatal Burden & Adhesive Contact Injury:** Premature infants (< 37 weeks gestational age) in the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) require continuous respiratory rate ($\brRef$) and apnea surveillance (`krbec2024emerging`, `senechal2026next`). Standard wired adhesive ECG/impedance pneumography electrodes damage fragile premature skin, provoke handling stress, and generate high false-alarm burdens under movement.
- **Promise & Physical Vulnerability of 24 GHz CW Doppler Radar:** Continuous-wave (CW) quadrature radar at $\carrierFreq = 24\,\mathrm{GHz}$ ($\radarLambda = 12.5\,\mathrm{mm}$) senses sub-millimeter thoracic excursions ($\chestDisp$) completely through clothing, swaddles, and incubator mattresses (`beltrao2022contactless`, `droitcour2004range`, `li2013review`, `gleichauf2021automated`, `lee2020feasibility`, `liao2025noncontact`). However, as systematized across 60 observational NICU studies (`senechal2026next`), clinical translation remains stalled by three coupled failures: (i) low-frequency cardiac/harmonic confusion and random body movement (RBM), (ii) methodological leakage and unclustered evaluation (`bland2007agreement`), and (iii) uncalibrated uncertainty during quiet apnea pauses vs. out-of-distribution (OOD) sensor corruption.

## 1.2 Problem Statement: Four Interlocking Failures in Machine Learning for Radar Vital Signs
1. **Unverified Baseline Reproducibility & Synchronization Leakage (`WP0`):**
   - Using cached branch-specific NMF lags inflates/distorts session errors (Session `12.1` drops `18.63 -> 3.47 bpm` under branch-neutral synchronization; `pool30_sync` HPSS+NLS moves `8.0854 -> 7.8173 bpm`), while naive window-pooled statistics ignore clustering across $S=28$ synced sessions / $K=11$ infants (`bland2007agreement`). Furthermore, the dataset exposes two unaligned clinical references: a smoothed `1 Hz` monitor trend (`br_1hz_30s`) trailing raw physical breath triggers (`1024 Hz Trig`) by `~10 s` and diverging during `>5 s` quiet breathing pauses (`1581/6640` windows, `23.8%` of `pool30_sync`).
2. **Causal Latency vs. Shortcut Memorization (`WP1–WP3`):**
   - Beltrão et al.'s (2022) `0`-parameter `HPSS+NLS` pipeline (`R0`, $\rmseSess = 7.8173\,\mathrm{bpm}$ on `1 Hz`, `8.7268 bpm` on `Trig -10s`) treats each $30\,\mathrm{s}$ window independently without temporal continuity, yet arbitrary black-box neural sequence models (`Ridge-439`, `MLP-15k`, `TCN-16k`) risk memorizing monitor smoothing and cohort mean BR (`~45–50 bpm`) instead of physical phase periodicity (`geirhos2020shortcut`).
3. **The Quiet-Apnea Safety vs. OOD Paradox in Selective Prediction (`WP4`):**
   - Memoryless spectral flatness/entropy (`U3`, `U4`) catches white-noise corruption (`0.980 / 0.974` AUROC) but falsely rejects genuine neonatal `>5 s` breathing pauses (`1.39x–1.42x > 1.15x` safety limit) because breath gaps flatten short-time spectra (`krbec2024emerging`). Meanwhile, fitted 5-parameter LOPO ridge SQI (`U6c`) also breaks pause safety (`1.23x > 1.15x`).
4. **Statistical Invalidity of Unaudited Conformal Intervals in Small-$K$ Clinical Cohorts:**
   - Standard split (`C1`) and LOPO window-pooled conformal (`C2`) violate exchangeability across correlated windows within each infant (`dunn2023distribution`), silently under-covering up to `5/11` infants (`min = 0.398` at `80%` target), whereas finite-$K$ patient-exchangeable conformal faces a hard mathematical floor $\alpha_{\min} = 1/(K_{\mathrm{cal}}+1) = 1/11 \approx 0.0909$, rendering $95\%$ prediction sets provably vacuous ($\mathrm{width} = \infty$) without $K_{\mathrm{cal}} \ge 19$ infants.

- **`RQ3` (Safe Selective Uncertainty & Finite-$K$ Conformal Bounds, Stage 3 / Ch. 5):** Can zero-parameter posterior spread (`U2_Posterior_SD`) and a zero-fitted-weight barrier composite (`U6b`) resolve the pause-safety vs. OOD paradox, and what are the exact mathematical coverage limits of conformal prediction sets at $K=11$ NICU patients (`C1–C5` vs. `Dunn et al. 2023`)?
