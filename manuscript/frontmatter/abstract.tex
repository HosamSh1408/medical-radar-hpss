\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}

{\emergencystretch=1.5em
Adhesive ECG and impedance leads injure fragile preterm skin in neonatal intensive care units
(NICUs). Contactless \SI{24}{\giga\hertz} continuous-wave (CW) Doppler radar senses
sub-millimeter thoracic motion through incubators, yet translation is hindered by
synchronization leakage, small-cohort shortcut learning, and uncalibrated uncertainty during
quiet breathing pauses.

This claim-gated thesis evaluates physics-constrained machine learning---encoding frequency
translation equivariance, $SO(2)\times\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ baseband invariance, and causal Markov
continuity---against unconstrained learned baselines. An internal forensic audit (\texttt{EXP-0};
$K=11$ infants, $S=28$ sessions, $|\mathcal{W}|=6640$ windows) removes branch-dependent lag
leakage, exposes an effective \SI{10}{\second} group delay between \SI{1}{\hertz} monitor trends
and \SI{1024}{\hertz} physical breath triggers, and documents $1581$ windows ($23.8\%$) with
quiet pauses exceeding \SI{5}{\second}. Controlled ablations retain locked complex demodulation
(\texttt{F0}) and Harmonic--Percussive Source Separation (\texttt{H0}; 64-tap Kaiser FIR
\SIrange{0.30}{3.00}{\hertz}, $\kaiserBeta=6.5$) over clipping/whitening and zero-lookahead
STFT variants on parsimony and equivalence grounds.

Across a four-rung complexity ladder trained solely on \SI{1}{\hertz} trends, the $8$-parameter
causal differentiable Hidden Markov Model (\texttt{FilterSafe-8}, \texttt{R2e}) lowers
equal-session $\rmseSess$ from memoryless \texttt{R0} (\SI{7.8173}{\bpm}) to \SI{6.8486}{\bpm}
($\Delta=-0.9687\,\mathrm{bpm}$, $11/11$ infants, $p_{\mathrm{pat,Holm}}=0.0117$) and is the
only causal model retaining Holm significance on lag-aligned physical triggers (\SI{8.7268} to
\SI{8.0634}{\bpm}, $10/11$ infants, $p_{\mathrm{pat,Holm}}=0.0215$). Unconstrained
$439$--$15{,}817$-parameter regressors (\texttt{Ridge}, \texttt{MLP}, \texttt{TCN}) score lower
on \SI{1}{\hertz} trends (\SIrange{5.7985}{6.5844}{\bpm}) via monitor-smoothing and prior
shortcuts, fail Holm significance on triggers ($p_{\mathrm{pat,Holm}}=1.00$), and degrade under
zero-touch transfer to an independent \SI{24.2}{\giga\hertz} neonatal phantom
(\texttt{gleichauf2024}, $20$ scenarios): \texttt{FilterSafe-8} attains \SI{0.0197}{\bpm}
in-band scenario RMSE (slope~$0.9994$, $0$ steady-state outliers $>\SI{0.5}{\bpm}$) versus
\SIrange{3.1782}{3.6353}{\bpm} in-band and \SIrange{11.7783}{32.7040}{\bpm} \SI{20}{\bpm}-skirt
errors for unconstrained baselines.

For uncertainty quantification, zero-parameter posterior spread
(\texttt{U2\_\allowbreak Posterior\_\allowbreak SD}) lowers selective risk across $10/11$
infants ($\eaurcMetric_{\mathrm{sess}}=-0.2454\,\mathrm{bpm}$, raw $p_{\mathrm{pat}}=0.0098$,
Holm $0.0781$) while staying pause-safe ($1.02\times$ relative rejection), whereas spectral
entropies ($1.39\text{--}1.42\times$) and a $5$-parameter regularized linear composite
(\texttt{U6c}, $1.23\times$, \SI{1}{\hertz} $95\%$ CI crossing zero) breach the $1.15\times$
safety cap. A zero-fitted-weight heuristic barrier composite (\texttt{U6b},
$\tau_{\mathrm{ood}}=0.90$) balances pause safety ($1.13\times$) with out-of-distribution
detection ($0.9986$ WGN AUROC; $0.9998$ external stopband AUROC). Finally, a finite-$K$ conformal
audit proves $95\%$ patient-exchangeable split-conformal intervals vacuous at $K_{\mathrm{cal}}=10$
($\alpha_{\min}=1/11$), confirms pooled-window under-coverage, and quantifies via hierarchical
calibration (\texttt{C5}, $80\%$ mean width \SI{24.02}{\bpm}) the gap between verified hardware
transfer (\texttt{L4}) and bedside clinical readiness (\texttt{L5}).
